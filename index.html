About Onboarded ↗
What is onboarding orchestration?

It coordinates the tasks, requirements, people, and systems involved in getting a worker ready to start and remain eligible to work.

Who is this kind of platform for?

It is especially relevant to organizations with high hiring volumes, distributed teams, changing requirements, or complex compliance processes.

Does it replace an ATS or HR system?

An orchestration layer is designed to connect operational steps across a technology ecosystem, rather than necessarily replacing every system already in use. Confirm the specific integration options with the vendor.

Can requirements vary by role or location?

Workflows can be designed to account for different roles, locations, and policy conditions. The exact capabilities depend on the product configuration.

Where can I find official product and compliance information?

Visit the official platform page, compliance page, or documentation for current details.
